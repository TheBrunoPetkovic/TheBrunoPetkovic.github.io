Swaypoint

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Swaypoint is an iPhone app for planning trips with friends: everyone swipes on places, and the app builds a plan from what the group likes. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

Swaypoint is made by Bruno Petković, Zagreb, Croatia (“we”). For anything about your data, email bruno.petkovic.business@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account: a random user ID. If you sign in, also your email address and, from Apple or Google, the name and email they share with us. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email.To keep your trips in your account and let you sign in on another phone.
Trips: the city, dates, trip length and pace, the places in the deck, your display name, and the trip's invite code.To run the trip and show it to the people you invite.
Votes: which places you swiped want, skip or must-see on, and whether you finished swiping.To find the places your group agrees on and build the plan.
Taste profile: the interests you pick and the places you rate. Today this stays on your phone.To put places you are likely to enjoy first.
Purchases: whether you have Swaypoint Pro or a Trip Pass. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card details.To unlock paid features.

People on the same trip can see each other's display names, progress and votes. Nobody else can: the database only returns a trip to its members.

We do not collect your location, contacts or photos. We do not show ads, sell data, or track you across other apps and websites.

Legal basis

We process this data to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and to keep the service secure and working (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)).

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts, trips and votes on servers in the European Union.
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store payments) and Google (Sign in with Google) when you choose to use them.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and the invite pages.

If we add new services that receive personal data, for example to generate itineraries with AI, we will update this policy first.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and trips until you delete them. When the organizer deletes a trip, its members and votes are deleted too. When you leave a trip, your membership and votes for that trip are deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. To delete everything, email us from the address on your account, or with your trip's invite code if you used Swaypoint as a guest. You can also complain to the Croatian data protection authority (AZOP, azop.hr) or the authority where you live.

Children

Swaypoint is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access is limited per user and per trip. No system is perfectly secure, so we also keep what we collect to a minimum.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
