Moxu

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Moxu is an iPhone app with an AI tutor. You tell it what you want to learn, it finds out what you already know, and it teaches you step by step, with questions and spaced review. This policy explains what data Moxu uses, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • You sign in with Apple. We get a random ID from Apple, not your password, and you can hide your email.
  • What you write or dictate in a lesson is sent to Anthropic's Claude to generate the tutor's replies. Anthropic does not use it to train its models.
  • Your voice stays on your iPhone. Dictation is turned into text on the device.
  • Your sessions and knowledge map are stored on servers in the EU so they sync and lessons can continue.
  • Basic usage statistics (which steps of the app people reach) are on by default, never include what you write, and can be turned off in Settings.
  • No ads, no selling data, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.

Who is responsible

Moxu is made by Bruno Petković, Zagreb, Croatia (“we”, “us”), who is the controller of your personal data. For anything about your data, email bruno.petkovic.business@gmail.com.

How the AI tutor works with your data

Moxu's tutor runs on Claude, made by Anthropic. When you take a lesson, our server sends Anthropic what it needs to write the next reply: your goal, the conversation so far in that session, your answers and confidence, the parts of your knowledge map you are practising, and summaries of materials you added. It does not send your name, email or Apple ID.

Anthropic processes this only to generate replies for us. Under its commercial terms it does not use this data to train its models, and it keeps it only for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. AI replies are generated automatically and can be wrong, so check anything important.

Moxu does not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. The AI only decides what to teach or ask you next.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): your account, lessons, knowledge map, materials, AI processing, Pro subscription and usage limits. Without them we can't provide Moxu.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): usage statistics to improve the app, device checks and limits to prevent abuse and keep costs sustainable, and keeping the records described under “How long we keep data”. You can object at any time, and you can switch off usage statistics in Settings.
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)): where we must keep or share data by law.

Services we use

We use these providers to run Moxu. Each receives only what it needs and processes it on our behalf under a data processing agreement.

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Anthropic (USA) runs the Claude AI models that write the tutor's replies and summarise materials.
  • RevenueCat (USA) manages subscription status. It receives your random Moxu user ID, your App Store purchase information and basic device information such as the iOS and app version, not your name or learning content.
  • PostHog (EU cloud, Germany) stores the usage statistics described above. IP addresses are discarded and no user profiles are created.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, App Attest, App Store payments and on-device speech recognition under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

When a link you add is fetched, the website you linked to sees a request from our server, not from you.

International transfers

Anthropic and RevenueCat are in the United States. When your data goes there, it is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Email us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

How long we keep data

  • Account, sessions, knowledge map, materials and lesson feedback: until you delete your account. Deleting the account removes them straight away.
  • Usage and cost records: when you delete your account, these records are kept without your user ID, so they no longer identify you. We use them for cost accounting and limits.
  • Free plan record: to stop the free plan being reset by deleting and recreating an account, we keep a one-way hash of your Sign in with Apple identifier together with how much of the free plan was used. It cannot be turned back into your Apple ID and is linked to nothing else.
  • Usage statistics: kept only as long as they are useful for comparing trends over time. They can't be linked to you, and the daily counts on our server contain no IDs at all.
  • Purchase records at Apple and RevenueCat: as required for accounting and tax law.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, restrict or object to how we use it, and withdraw any consent. Email us and we will reply within one month.

  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Settings › Delete account. This deletes your account, sessions, knowledge map and materials immediately. Deleting your account does not cancel a Moxu Pro subscription: cancel it in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • Stop usage statistics: Settings › Share usage statistics. This also deletes the analytics ID on your iPhone.
  • Stop using Sign in with Apple for Moxu: iPhone Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple.

You can also complain to the Croatian data protection authority (AZOP, azop.hr) or the authority in the country where you live.

Children

Moxu is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 16, or under the age of digital consent in your country, please use Moxu only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections and stored encrypted at rest by our providers. Your data is kept in a private part of the database that only our server can reach, and every request must come from your signed-in account and the genuine app. No system is perfectly secure, so we also keep what we collect to a minimum.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. For important changes, for example a new provider that receives your learning content, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
