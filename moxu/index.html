Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email bruno.petkovic.business@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened, and we'll usually reply within two working days.

What's free?

Your personal study plan and your first lesson. After that, Moxu Pro keeps the tutor going.

What does Moxu Pro include?

Up to 500 tutor turns per subscription month, across as many courses as you like. A turn is one reply from the tutor. The allowance refills each month; Settings shows how many you have left.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Moxu. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it

Open Moxu › Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account and data?

In Moxu, open Settings › Delete account. This removes your account, sessions and knowledge map from our servers immediately. It doesn't cancel a subscription, so cancel that separately in iPhone Settings.

What happens to what I write?

Your answers are sent to the AI (Anthropic's Claude) to write the tutor's replies, and stored on EU servers so your lessons sync. Dictation is turned into text on your iPhone. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
