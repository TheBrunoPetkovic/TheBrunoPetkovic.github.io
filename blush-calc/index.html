Privacy

Calculations, calculation history, lists, and theme preference stay on your device. Blush Calc does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics, and does not ask for an account.

Purchases. Blush Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. To check whether you have access to Blush Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history from Apple (product, price, dates, renewal status), and basic device information such as the app version, operating system, and region. This data is used only to provide and restore your purchases and to show the developer aggregated sales information. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

We do not collect your name, email address, or payment details. If this policy changes, the updated policy will be published on this page.

Subscriptions

Blush Pro is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, each with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Use of the app is subject to the Apple Standard End User License Agreement.
